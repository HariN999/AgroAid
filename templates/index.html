Best Scanning Practices

  • Focus clearly on the leaf: Avoid shadows, background soil clutter, or branch overlap.
  • Ensure good lighting: Use natural diffused sunlight. Avoid overexposure or dark spots.
  • Crop target details: Position the diseased area/leaf spot in the center of the image.
  • High resolution uploads: Pillow transforms will format shapes automatically, but details must be sharp.

General Crop Prevention

  • Crop Rotation: Disrupt cycles of soilborne pathogens by changing crop families annually.
  • Drip Irrigation: Water the root zones directly. Wet leaves encourage fungal mold spores.
  • Sanitation: Prune and burn infected branch foliage immediately. Never compost diseased plant waste.
  • Air Circulation: Space rows properly to prevent micro-humidity build-ups.
